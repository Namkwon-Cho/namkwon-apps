# Minzzock Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026

Minzzock collects no personal information. There is no account, server of its own, analytics, ads or tracking, and saved recipes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

## What is collected

Minzzock collects no personal information such as your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers, and nothing is sent to the developer.

The app has no account, server of its own, analytics, ads or tracking.

## What is stored on the device

Saved recipes (titles, ingredients, steps, photos, sources), your notes and cooking log, and app settings are stored only on this device.

Deleting the app deletes what it stored on the device. What's kept in iCloud can be removed in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

## iCloud sync and backups

With iCloud on, recipes and photos are stored in your own Apple account's iCloud (private database and iCloud Drive) and synced between devices on that account.

Apple keeps this information, and the developer can't see it. A backup file you make holds your recipes and photos, and where you keep or share it is up to you.

## Internet use

The built-in browser and recipe saving connect from your device directly to the websites you open, whose own privacy and cookie policies apply. Recipe photos are downloaded from the original site as well.

‘Today's pick’ on the home screen loads 10000recipe's recommendation page directly on your device. Minzzock isn't affiliated with 10000recipe.

With ‘Reduce web ads’ on in settings, ads are reduced on this device only, using the public ad-blocking list (EasyList) built into the app; browsing history is never sent anywhere. It's off by default.

## Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when you turn on voice commands in cook mode. Speech is recognized on the device and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone turns off when the app goes to the background.

## Importing from other apps and sharing

Other recipe apps' export files are read only on this device and never sent anywhere.

‘Add to Minzzock’ in the share sheet passes the page you share to Minzzock through the app's shared space on this device.

## Crash reports

Only if you allow ‘Share with App Developers’ in your device settings does Apple pass anonymous crash and diagnostic information to the developer. You can change this at any time.

## Children

Minzzock collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the app and this page show the new text and its effective date.
