# Minzzock Support

Minzzock is a personal recipe keeper that saves recipes you find on recipe sites, blogs and YouTube with their photos, exactly as written.

## Contact

If something isn't working or you have a suggestion, please email us.

- Email: [Namkwon.App@gmail.com](mailto:Namkwon.App@gmail.com)

It helps to include your device model, iOS version, the app version (Settings › About Minzzock) and the address of the recipe page where the problem happened.

## FAQ

**A recipe won't save.**
The site's layout may have changed, or the page may not contain a recipe. Please send the error shown in the preview and the page address.

**Recipes don't show up on my other device.**
Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account and that Minzzock is turned on in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

**Can I bring recipes from another recipe app?**
Yes. In Settings › Import from other apps, choose an export file from Paprika, Mela, Crouton or Recipe Keeper, or a recipe file.

**Can I change the app's language?**
Minzzock follows your device's language. You can also choose English or Korean in Settings › Language. Saved recipes always stay in their original language.
